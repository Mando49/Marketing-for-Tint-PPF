Why Tesla owners tint

A Tesla is mostly glass, and it lives in the desert.

A lot of glass

Model 3 and Model Y have far more glass area than a typical sedan or SUV, including the panoramic roof, one large overhead panel that sits right above your head.

Desert sun

Las Vegas summers put sun on your car all day, from overhead as much as from the sides. Parked outside, all that glass is what the heat comes through.

A cooler cabin

Film on the glass, the roof included, makes the cabin more comfortable to sit in and cuts the glare coming down through the roof on a bright day.

Cut to the panel

The roof is cut as one piece to that panel's shape, and the side and rear glass to your model. I can do the roof on its own or the whole car in one visit.

In Nevada, front side windows must allow more than 35% of light through. Darker shades like 15% and 5% are for rear side windows and back glass only. I'll walk you through what's legal for your vehicle.
