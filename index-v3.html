Tesla window tint in Las Vegas →

Glass roof tinting for Model 3 and Model Y

The panoramic roof on a Model 3 or Model Y is one large overhead glass panel, and it sits directly over you. Film on that panel makes the cabin more comfortable in the Las Vegas heat and cuts the overhead glare.

The roof is cut as one piece to the panel's shape and installed by appointment, same as any other glass on the car. We do the side and rear glass at the same visit if you want it.

Automotive window tint

Global window tint film applied to your vehicle's glass to cut heat and glare and keep the interior from baking. Anything from a light film to the darkest look you want.

Paint protection film

LLumar paint protection film — clear film over your paint that takes the rock chips, sand, and road debris instead of your finish. Partial or full coverage.

Matte paint protection film

Clear film that protects your paint and changes the finish to matte at the same time.

Old tint removal

Failed, purple, or bubbling film taken off without damaging your door panels, defroster lines, or glass. Proper removal is what makes a re-tint come out right.

Residential window tint

Tint for your home's windows to bring down cooling bills and keep the afternoon sun off your furniture and floors.
